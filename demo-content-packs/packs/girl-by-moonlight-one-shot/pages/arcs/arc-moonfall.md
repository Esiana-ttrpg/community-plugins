# Moonfall Arc

The eclipse broke more than a ritual — it broke the city's count. [[obj-lunar-idol]] vanished from [[loc-old-shrine]] while official reports insist five witnesses stood where the coven remembers four. Every faction that hunts the relic hunts a different ending: containment, leverage, or open harvest.

This arc tracks the coven from [[event-recent-eclipse]] through [[quest-find-idol]] and [[quest-investigate-ruins]] toward [[quest-confront-regent]], while [[thread-hope-vs-despair]] and [[thread-city-secret]] ask whether saving Lunar City can also save the people the girls love — and whether the fifth name on the founding plaque still has a holder walking the streets.

When the party gathers in [[scene-yuki-room-gathering]], the arc turns from chase to choice: trust Yuki's badge, burn the Directorate's files, or follow the fifth silhouette on the shrine footage into whatever the city erased.
