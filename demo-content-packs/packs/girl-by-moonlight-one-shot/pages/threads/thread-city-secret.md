# The City's Secret

**Episode hook:** Mira compares three official lists side by side on the council room floor — the eclipse incident report (five witnesses), the shrine volunteer roster (four names), and the founding plaque rubbing from [[event-founding-ritual]] (five participants, one chiseled away). The numbers do not add up, and every adult she asks changes the subject to curfew compliance.

Run this as a investigation beat between action scenes: a librarian who remembers a different school song lyric, a janitor who swears there used to be a fifth locker in the shrine annex, Yuki's redacted briefing with the same paragraph blacked out twice.

The city is lying about how many girls the moon chose. The coven feels like four — so who is the fifth witness, and why does Lunar City still need them gone?
