# Hope vs Despair

**Episode hook:** Hana's sister stops eating. Not dramatically — she just picks at lunch and asks when the moon will "finish coming back." Hana wants to skip the ruin crawl and sit with her; Mira says the idol is the only medicine that works. Play the scene at the Ashvale apartment door: does Hana go with the coven tonight, or does someone else promise what she cannot yet deliver?

The rooftop confession in [[scene-rooftop-confession]] opened this wound. Ren offered to carry the guilt; Hana heard abandonment. Every quest clock that ticks is also a clock on a child's trust.

When the party chooses mission over family — or family over mission — mark which side of the thread gained ground.
