# Resolved Oath

**Episode hook:** *(Already played — use as callback, not cliffhanger.)* On the first night after the coven formed, the four of them swore on the rooftop haven that no secret would leave the circle without consent. Ren broke a Syndicate tail alone; when she came back bleeding, she told the truth before anyone asked. That oath held through [[quest-media-leak]].

If trust fractures later, invoke this memory: someone kept a promise once when it hurt. The city secret may demand a new oath — or prove the old one was always incomplete because it counted four mouths, not five.
