# Future Convergence

*(GM foreshadow — has not happened in play.)*

Directorate predictive models — which Yuki has not yet seen — mark a **second total eclipse** within the one-shot's horizon if the idol stays missing. Convergence charts overlay five ley peaks: [[loc-old-shrine]], [[loc-ruined-observatory]], [[loc-directorate-tower]], [[loc-shirogane-campus]], and a sealed point beneath the bay labeled *Fifth Witness*.

On that night, the binding text in [[lore-moon-curse]] completes whether or not the coven is ready: five bearers, one gate, one disappearance. The Syndicate plans a public rite at the observatory dome. The Regent prepares an address offering "managed parity." Ren's Resistance cell is split between smashing the idol permanently and returning it with a fifth signature appended.

If the party does nothing, the convergence resolves the Fifth Witness mystery the cruel way — the city learns the count aloud, and someone not at the table pays the disappearer's price.

Use this event to pressure clocks, not to railroad outcomes. The convergence is a **deadline**, not a prophecy.
