# Recent Eclipse

Three weeks ago Lunar City held its breath under a total eclipse — the kind calendars predict but bodies remember as **wrong**. Streetlights came on early. Phones captured a dim red corona over the academy spires. In the coven's private account, the night divides into before and after the shrine.

The girls gathered at [[loc-old-shrine]] with [[obj-lunar-idol]] at the center of a containment circle. They speak of four voices finishing the opening verse. They do not agree on what happened when the verse failed — only that light folded inward, Hana collapsed, and the idol was gone when anyone could see again.

Public aftermath arrived faster than dawn. The Directorate issued a calm-down bulletin: atmospheric refraction, no cause for alarm. [[org-shirogane-academy]] canceled morning classes "for safety review." A curfew map appeared on kiosks with the shrine district shaded red.

What the party has seen with their own eyes: civilian masks slipping in the eclipse shadow; a surge of moon-touched dreams across the dorm; Syndicate scouts near the ruined observatory within hours.

What they have **paper** for: an incident summary Mira scraped from a council printer — official, stamped, dry. It lists property damage at the shrine annex, unauthorized assembly, and **five witnesses** present during the anomaly window. Four names match the coven. The fifth is typed as *UNKNOWN ADJACENT*, with a note to disregard as lens flare. [[pc-yuki]] received a matching briefing with the fifth line blacked out entirely.

The eclipse did not start the war. It **timestamped** the lie the city was already telling about how many girls the moon can hold at once.
