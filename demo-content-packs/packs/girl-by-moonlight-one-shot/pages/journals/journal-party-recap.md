## After the Rooftop

We met at [[haven-school-rooftop|School Rooftop]] after curfew — the only place on [[loc-shirogane-campus|Shirogane Campus]] the Directorate cameras don't quite reach. Wind off the city, antenna lights blinking like slow pulse. [[pc-hana|Hana]] and [[pc-ren|Ren]] got there first. The rest of us followed in ones and twos so it wouldn't look like a conspiracy from the quad.

**What happened:** Ren finally said out loud what we'd been circling for a week — that the Resistance didn't steal the [[obj-lunar-idol|Lunar Idol]], but someone in our orbit might have moved it to keep the Directorate from locking the vault forever. Hana didn't deny it. She couldn't. Her hands shook on the rail until Ren put a jacket over them and told her to breathe. Not romantic. Not yet. Just *witness*.

Hana admitted her stake isn't abstract. Her sister Sora has been sleepwalking through moonlit halls, humming a melody that matches the eclipse broadcast glitch. If the curse is choosing vessels, Hana is done pretending it's a metaphor.

Mira wanted every name on record. Yuki wanted every name *off* record. They argued in whispers until I (Ren) reminded them the Herald has drones now. We compromised: Mira gets a redacted timeline; Yuki gets twenty-four hours to verify before it goes to council.

**The secret we uncovered:** Someone took a photo from the adjacent tower — blurry, timestamp 03:16, five figures on the rooftop. We only account for four. The fifth silhouette is shorter, halo-shaped blur around the head. Hana went white. Ren deleted the metadata and kept the file. We're calling it the **Fifth Witness** until we know otherwise.

**If nobody intervenes:** The photo leaks anyway, tagged as "coven ritual," and the Directorate uses it to justify campus lockdown. The Fifth Witness becomes a hunt target — or a scapegoat.
