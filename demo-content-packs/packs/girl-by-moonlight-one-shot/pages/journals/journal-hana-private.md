I told them I'm looking for the idol because the city needs it back.

That's true enough to pass a lie detector I don't have.

The real reason is Sora.

My little sister knew where it was before anyone announced it was missing.

She shouldn't have known.

---

Day after the eclipse she came to my room at 2 AM.

Not crying. Worse — *quiet*.

She asked if the "silver lady" was still in the observatory photos.

I said what everyone said: hoax, compression artifact, Directorate psy-op.

She looked at me like I'd read the wrong line in a play.

"Sora-chan, where did you hear that?"

"Nowhere."

She said it too fast.

---

I've been combing the shrine logs, the vault manifests, Mira's council subpoena drafts.

The idol left the vault at 03:14.

Sora's phone pinged near [[loc-old-shrine|Old Shrine]] at 03:11.

Three minutes.

Could be nothing.

Could be everything.

---

She's sixteen.

She's brilliant and soft and she flinches when the moon is too bright.

Mom calls it anemia.

I call it the [[lore-moon-curse|Moon Curse]] and swallow the word because saying it out loud makes it real.

Sora drew the fractured halo in her notebook before the Herald published the photo.

I found the sketch under her mattress.

She said she dreamed it.

---

If the idol is gone, the binding weakens.

If the binding weakens, the curse looks for a vessel.

House [[family-shirogane|Shirogane]] has guarded that vault for four generations.

What if the vault wasn't protecting the idol?

What if it was protecting *us*?

---

Ren says the Resistance has names for girls who "brighten" during eclipses.

I haven't told the coven about Sora's ping.

Not yet.

Mira would turn it into a headline.

Yuki would file a report.

Ren would go still in that way that means someone might disappear.

---

I'm on [[quest-find-idol|Find the Missing Idol]] because I need the truth before the truth finds my sister.

If nobody intervenes, Sora becomes a footnote in a Directorate containment memo.

I won't let that happen.

Even if the girl who took the idol is the girl I tuck in at night.

Especially then.

---

Tonight [[pc-yuki|Yuki]]'s room. All four of us.

We say it's going to be fine.

I'm going to smile when I say it.

I'm not going to believe it.

But I'll show up.

That's what big sisters do.

We carry the secret until we can put it down without dropping someone.

I haven't found a place to put this one yet.
