# Arcade Loft

Behind a shuttered rhythm-game cabinet on the edge of [[loc-shirogane-campus|Shirogane Campus]] commercial strip, a narrow service ladder climbs into a loft the arcade owner pretends not to know about. Dust motes hang in the light of a single string of festival bulbs. Empty crates form seating. A portable heater fights the winter draft with more optimism than success. This is the **Arcade Loft** — a hideout haven, strained and small, where the coven does work too messy or too loud for the [[haven-school-rooftop|School Rooftop]] sanctuary.

The loft's discovery state is **known** to the inner circle and a handful of sympathetic shopkeepers who remember when the Resistance paid in cash and didn't ask for receipts. Its arcane theme manifests in salvaged parts: telescope gears from a junk bin, copper wire traced in patterns Mira copied from pre-binding diagrams, a corkboard map linking [[loc-ruined-observatory|Ruined Observatory]] sightlines to [[loc-broadcast-tower|Broadcast Tower]] transmission windows. [[project-repair-observatory|Repair the Observatory]] lives here in crates of rusted bolts and handwritten inventories — thirty-five percent complete and climbing whenever someone smuggles another component past campus security.

Strained means resources, not morale. Power comes from an extension cord that trips if anyone runs the heater and the soldering iron together. Water is a floor below, in a staff sink that locks after ten. [[pc-hana|Hana Shirogane]] still holds guardian duty on paper, but the loft's real keeper is whoever is mid-project — lately [[pc-mira|Mira Kaito]], who treats the space like a newsroom and a chapel combined.

The reinforced rail upgrade borders on absurd up here — there is barely a landing — but Hana insisted after a near-fall during a midnight haul. Hideouts teach paranoia; prosperous havens teach hope. The loft teaches both at once.

When the [[org-eclipse-syndicate|Eclipse Syndicate]] tightens surveillance on [[haven-ashvale-apartment|Ashvale Apartment]], meetings migrate here. When the Directorate schedules another "wellness sweep," telescope parts hide under tarps that smell like old popcorn grease. The loft is not comfortable. It is necessary — the workshop where the city’s lies get taken apart piece by piece, one stripped screw at a time.
