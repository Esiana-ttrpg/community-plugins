# Repair the Observatory

The [[loc-ruined-observatory|Ruined Observatory]] did not collapse in one dramatic night. It failed in stages — funding cuts, "structural concerns," a sealed dome, then silence — until the city learned to treat it as background ruin, the way you stop noticing a crack in pavement. The [[event-recent-eclipse|Recent Eclipse]] shattered that complacency. Someone stood on that roof with arms raised and a halo like cracked glass, and the broadcast clip made the observatory impossible to ignore again.

[[pc-mira|Mira Kaito]] owns **Repair the Observatory**, active at thirty-five percent, staged from [[haven-arcade-loft|Arcade Loft]] where salvaged gears and annotated schematics cover every flat surface. The goal is not nostalgia. A working observatory means sightlines — proof of who could have accessed the dome, when the [[obj-lunar-idol|Lunar Idol]] vault was last physically reachable, whether [[loc-broadcast-tower|Broadcast Tower]] relays align with the eclipse interval [[pc-hana|Hana Shirogane]]'s sister Sora keeps describing with unsettling precision.

High priority because the [[org-lunar-directorate|Lunar Directorate]] already circulates language about "scheduled relocation" and "public safety closures." If they seal the site first, the coven inherits a story without evidence. If the Resistance reaches it first, the story becomes actionable — or explosive. [[org-crescent-resistance|Crescent Resistance]] contacts have smuggled bolts and lens housings; [[pc-ren|Ren Ashvale]] has family maps that predate the ruin; [[pc-yuki|Yuki Arden]] walks a knife-edge between agency loyalty and feeding Mira intel she shouldn't have.

No formal blockers sit on the ledger. Informal ones abound: campus security rotations, Syndicate scouts near the access road, and the [[beast-moon-hound|Moon Hound]] rumors that keep volunteers from signing up twice. Mira works anyway. Each repaired bracket is a argument against the Directorate narrative. Each night on the loft corkboard connects [[loc-old-shrine|Old Moon Shrine]] tradition to hardware someone tried to erase.

The project is active because the moonfall arc demands a place where the sky can be read honestly again — and because Mira refuses to let the city's most important witness stay a prop in someone else's cover-up.
