# Research Ancient Relics

[[pc-mira|Mira Kaito]] did not pause this project because she lost interest. She paused it because the [[org-shirogane-academy|Shirogane Academy]] archive wing changed its access policy forty-eight hours after the [[obj-lunar-idol|Lunar Idol]] theft, and the one key that opens the pre-binding stacks — the drawer labeled *Lunar Regents, Restricted* — vanished from the faculty checkout log the same night someone in Directorate livery toured the basement.

At thirty-five percent, the work is already dangerous. Mira has cross-indexed shrine inventories from the [[loc-old-shrine|Old Moon Shrine]] with observatory ledgers from before the [[loc-ruined-observatory|Ruined Observatory]] collapse. She has photographed rubbings that match symbols on the idol's empty vault pedestal. She has a working theory that the [[lore-moon-curse|Moon Curse]] is not a single spell but a chain of containment artifacts, each one failing in sequence — a theory [[org-lunar-directorate|Lunar Directorate]] public-relations would call "unverified speculation" if they acknowledged it at all.

The project is anchored at [[haven-school-rooftop|School Rooftop]], where printouts spread across tar paper and get weighted with stones so the wind cannot steal them. High priority because every faction clock is running: the [[org-crescent-resistance|Crescent Resistance]] wants ammunition; the [[org-eclipse-syndicate|Eclipse Syndicate]] wants the same documents for different reasons; [[pc-hana|Hana Shirogane]] needs to know whether Sora's eclipse memories align with archive fact or something worse.

The blocker is blunt: **missing archive key**. Without it, the next tranche of primary sources stays locked — including, Mira suspects, a binding oath register that names [[family-ashvale|House Ashvale]] alongside institutions the city claims are unrelated. She has filed three public-records requests. She has been ignored three times.

Paused is not abandoned. Mira keeps a list of who had key access, who transferred shifts, who benefitted from the eclipse distraction. When the key surfaces — or when someone foolish enough to use it leaves a trail — Research Ancient Relics resumes. Until then, the coven operates on fragments, and fragments are how cover-ups survive.
