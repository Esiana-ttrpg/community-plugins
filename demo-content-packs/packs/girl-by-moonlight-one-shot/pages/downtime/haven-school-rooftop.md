# School Rooftop

Above the [[loc-shirogane-campus|Shirogane Campus]] gymnasium, a service stairwell ends at a door students are told not to use. The lock was changed the semester before the eclipse; the key is shared now among four names and one emergency duplicate taped inside a poetry anthology no one reads. This is the **School Rooftop** haven — prosperous by coven standards, which means the wind still bites but the rail holds, the tar patches don't leak when it rains, and nobody has been ambushed here yet.

[[pc-hana|Hana Shirogane]] claimed guardian role the night after the [[event-recent-eclipse|Recent Eclipse]], when half the dormitory reported strange sightings and the Directorate sent "wellness counselors" who asked the wrong questions. The rooftop became anchor by necessity: visible enough to feel safe, elevated enough to see sirens before they arrive, close enough to [[org-shirogane-academy|Shirogane Academy]] archives that [[pc-mira|Mira Kaito]] can descend with a stack of forbidden indices and return before curfew.

The haven's arcane temperament shows in small ways. Chalk sigils under the water tank, refreshed on new moons. A tin box of offerings — convenience-store rice crackers, mostly — left where the old shrine tradition said hungry spirits gather. The reinforced rail upgrade was Hana's first act as guardian: not glamour, just steel and bolts, after Sora admitted she dreamed of falling from heights that weren't in any building she knew.

[[scene-rooftop-confession|Rooftop Confession]] happened here — the scene that tied [[quest-find-idol|Find the Missing Idol]] to [[thread-hope-vs-despair|Hope vs Despair]] when [[pc-ren|Ren Ashvale]] finally said aloud what the Ashvale compact forbade. That memory lingers in the air like ozone. The rooftop is where the coven checks in after dark, where [[pc-yuki|Yuki Arden]] can remove her agency badge and still be Yuki, where plans for [[project-relic-research|Research Ancient Relics]] get debated between bites of convenience-store bread.

Prosperous does not mean permanent. The campus may seal. The Directorate may reclaim "unused facilities." For now, the rooftop is the one place four girls agreed the moon could wait outside the door — and for a few hours, it listens.
