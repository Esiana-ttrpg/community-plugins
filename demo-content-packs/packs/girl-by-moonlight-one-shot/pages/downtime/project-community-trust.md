# Build Community Trust

When [[org-eclipse-syndicate|Eclipse Syndicate]] surveillance tightened on [[haven-ashvale-apartment|Ashvale Apartment]], [[pc-mira|Mira Kaito]] argued that countersurveillance alone would fail — neighbors already whispered about girls in academy blazers visiting at odd hours. Trust, she said, was camouflage. Not friendship for its own sake, though some of that happened anyway, but a mesh of small obligations that made reporting the Ashvale flat feel like betraying people who had fixed your intercom.

The project ran out of the apartment haven at **one hundred percent** completion because Mira approached it like journalism with receipts. She documented every favor exchanged: borrowed sugar returned with interest, a super's stuck lock repaired before he asked, trash carried down on mornings [[pc-ren|Ren Ashvale]] couldn't face the hallway. [[pc-hana|Hana Shirogane]] contributed presence — guardian duty reframed as "cousin visiting from out of town" — and [[pc-yuki|Yuki Arden]] quietly ensured no Directorate "wellness" flyers appeared on the lobby board.

High priority because the [[event-recent-eclipse|Recent Eclipse]] turned Lunar City into a rumor economy overnight. [[org-city-herald|The City Herald]] ran moon-sickness pieces. Students posted clips faster than fact-checkers could breathe. In that climate, a threatened haven without neighborhood cover becomes a pin on someone's map. Mira intended to prevent the pin.

Completion does not mean immunity. Trust built slowly can collapse in one siren, one viral clip, one Syndicate agent asking the bakery owner questions she already answered. The project's legacy is breathing room: a week, maybe two, before neighbors decide curiosity outweighs gratitude. That window let [[family-ashvale|House Ashvale]] host coven debriefs while [[project-repair-observatory|Repair the Observatory]] smuggled parts through the commercial district.

Mira keeps a ledger still — names, favors, who owes whom — not because the project failed, but because completed operations in a city like this are perishable goods. The coven won a rare thing: time. They spent it immediately.
