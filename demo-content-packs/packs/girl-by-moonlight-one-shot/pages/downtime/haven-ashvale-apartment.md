# Ashvale Apartment

The Ashvale flat sits three floors above a bakery that opens before dawn, in a neighborhood the Directorate maps as "stable residential" and the Resistance maps as "containment-adjacent." From the street it is unremarkable — good locks, neutral curtains, no family crest on the buzzer. Inside, [[family-ashvale|House Ashvale]] keeps the accoutrements of normal life arranged with surgical care: textbooks on the table, headphones on the hook, nothing that suggests a [[anc-moon-touched|Moon-Touched]] heir lives here.

As a registered haven, the apartment serves a different function. [[pc-ren|Ren Ashvale]] opened it to the coven as a place to regroup away from campus cameras — a residence haven, small in scale, arcane in temperament. [[pc-hana|Hana Shirogane]] holds guardian duty on the roster, which means she is the one who checks the wards, swaps the hallway mirror that sometimes lagged after the [[event-recent-eclipse|Recent Eclipse]], and notices when the same unmarked sedan passes twice before lunch.

The haven is **threatened**. Syndicate surveillance has escalated from casual to deliberate: a new camera on the corner pole, a "maintenance worker" who asked the super about unit layouts, a faint scent of ozone in the stairwell that does not belong to any earthly repair. The reinforced rail upgrade — more habit than luxury in this building — now guards a balcony Ren uses for quiet conversations the rooftop cannot host.

[[project-community-trust|Build Community Trust]] completed here because neighbors needed reasons to look away from coven traffic, not because the Ashvales suddenly became beloved. Trust was purchased in borrowed sugar, fixed intercoms, and the unspoken agreement that nobody asks why girls with academy bags visit after midnight. That goodwill is fraying. Every day the [[org-eclipse-syndicate|Eclipse Syndicate]] watches longer, the apartment becomes less refuge and more pressure point — the kind of place where a family curse and a city-wide conspiracy intersect in a kitchen too small for either.

If the haven falls, Ren loses the last private room she has. If it holds, everyone who enters leaves a trace the Syndicate can follow. The coven meets here anyway. They have nowhere safer that is still theirs.
