# Directorate Tower

Directorate Tower is the spine of [[loc-lunar-city]]'s official story — a glass needle where [[org-lunar-directorate]] schedules eclipse advisories, issues curfew expansions, and stores the sealed files that contradict its own press releases. Visitors see lobbies of calm typography and civic murals. Staff see badge tiers, soundproof conference rooms, and basement archives labeled with the old name **Civic Watch Committee** before the rebranding made fear sound modern.

The tower's history is a history of containment. When the city first charted lunar anomalies, committees met here to translate prophecy into policy. When bindings failed and succeeded, minutes disappeared. [[lore-directorate-secret]] — the party's hidden mirror to public [[lore-moon-curse]] — lives in this building's logic: transformation as a problem to manage, not a life to honor. [[pc-yuki]] crosses the threshold wearing loyalty like a uniform while carrying friendships that would be classified if named aloud.

Motivation radiates downward from executive floors. Stability. Legitimacy. A narrative that keeps transit running and investors calm. After the [[event-recent-eclipse]], that motivation acquired teeth: emergency annexes, covert cooperation with Regent Vale's envoys, directives to treat [[org-crescent-resistance]] cells as unauthorized cults rather than frightened children. The tower does not hate the coven; it fears what honesty would cost.

Conflict is structural and daily. Press officers spar with [[org-city-herald]] editors by phone. Academy liaisons negotiate what [[org-shirogane-academy]] may omit from incident reports. Analysts map [[loc-ruined-observatory]] movements while publicly calling the site condemned. Every floor believes it protects the city. Few agree on from what.

Future pressure centers on [[event-future-convergence]]. Draft statutes already queued in legal review would make temporary eclipse powers permanent — arrests without public charges, seizures without warrants, broadcast kill switches tied to [[loc-broadcast-tower]]. If the party forces a confrontation here, the tower will not crumble; it will respond with the practiced calm of an institution that has survived scandal by renaming it. The question is whether [[pc-yuki]]'s conscience can survive inside the elevator music long enough to change what happens before the moon falls again.
