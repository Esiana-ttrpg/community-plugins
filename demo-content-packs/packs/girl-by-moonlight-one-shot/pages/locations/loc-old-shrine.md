# Old Moon Shrine

Old Moon Shrine lies beneath the burned shell of [[loc-ruined-observatory]] — older than the dome, older than the city's proudest lie of being founded free of superstition. Where the public ruin ends in charred beams, stone steps descend into a chamber that does not appear on Directorate tourism maps. Moonlight enters through cracks engineered on purpose, not by accident. Offerings here were never flowers and coins alone; they were names, oaths, and the first bargains that became [[lore-moon-curse]].

The shrine remembers [[event-founding-ritual]] in its geometry: circles carved to bind transformation to consent, or so the Resistance's stolen marginalia claims. [[org-eclipse-syndicate]] reads the same carvings as license — proof that power belongs to those bold enough to seize convergence. Regent Vale's couriers move relic fragments toward this threshold because [[event-future-convergence]] is not an abstract calendar entry here; it is acoustics, the way the chamber hums when the moon's phase matches the stone.

For decades the shrine slept under rubble and denial. Faculty at [[org-shirogane-academy]] taught the binding as literary metaphor. [[org-lunar-directorate]] sealed surveys after the fire. Then the [[event-recent-eclipse]] woke the lower halls — cold light, whispered syllables in languages no living club teaches. [[org-crescent-resistance]] scouts who reached the antechamber returned shaking, not from combat, from recognition: the curse they live is not random mutation but inherited jurisprudence.

Motivation differs by who stands on the steps. The Resistance wants to reclaim the shrine as sanctuary — a place where girls choose rather than are chosen. Vale wants to finish what the first binders started with modern replacements for ancient sacrifices. The Directorate wants the shrine to remain a rumor because admission would collapse property law, medical ethics, and a century of eclipse advisories in one filing.

Conflict is imminent. [[obj-lunar-idol]]'s absence aches in the chamber like a missing tooth. [[loc-ashvale-apartment]] oaths and [[family-ashvale]] bloodline lore both point here, tying [[pc-ren]]'s fate to stone that predates her building's elevator. **Sora**, still untransformed, represents the stake: if binding returns as public policy, shrines stop being history and become assembly lines.

Future pressure is singular. When convergence arrives, this room will not host debate. It will host execution of a ritual design — hope versus despair made architectural. The Old Moon Shrine is the city's memory made physical, and every faction in [[loc-lunar-city]] is running out of time to decide whether that memory should be healed, harnessed, or buried again under fire-scarred lies.
