# Ruined Observatory

The Ruined Observatory crowns the western ridge of [[loc-lunar-city]] like a broken cup turned to the sky. Generations ago it was a public temple to astronomy — school trips, donor plaques, the respectable face of looking upward. Then fire took the dome and the city decided mourning was expensive. Gates rusted. Warning signs faded. Official maps marked the site condemned while unofficial maps marked it **forbidden**.

The observatory was abandoned after the fire, but abandonment did not mean emptiness. Since the [[event-recent-eclipse]], neighbors report lights moving inside the shattered dome — not flashlights, not vandals, patterns that pulse like breathing. [[org-crescent-resistance]] believes the ruin hides a **Binding Ritual site** older than the academy's textbooks admit: stone aligned to [[event-founding-ritual]] geometry, reachable through collapsed maintenance shafts that [[project-repair-observatory]] slowly clears from the [[haven-arcade-loft]] side of planning.

[[org-eclipse-syndicate]] claims the ruin as headquarters in the only sense that matters — Regent Vale's cells rotate through its tunnels, staging relic recoveries bound for [[loc-old-shrine]] before [[event-future-convergence]]. Vale's people treat the fire as a curtain, not a tragedy. [[org-lunar-directorate]] patrols pass on schedule with suspicious gaps. [[org-shirogane-academy]] faculty still call Syndicate stories urban legend, yet no teacher volunteers to chaperone the ridge path after dusk.

Motivation collides here because the ruin is a keyhole. Whoever controls the binding site controls the next chapter of [[lore-moon-curse]] — whether transformation remains a scattered private fate or becomes civic engineering again. [[quest-investigate-ruins]] is active because the coven smelled ozone and incense on the wind the night [[obj-lunar-idol]] went missing. [[beast-moon-hound]] tracks seen at the tree line suggest the ruin is not uninhabited; it is guarded.

Conflict is imminent and layered. Resistance scouts want to seal the shafts. Mira's council allies want evidence before the Herald publishes. Yuki's Directorate maps may already show rooms the public plans omit. Future pressure is convergence: if Vale completes a ritual here, the city's hidden war ends not with compromise but with binding — names written into stone while [[pc-hana]]'s sister **Sora** sleeps unsuspecting in a dormitory that can see this hill on clear nights.

The Ruined Observatory is not a backdrop. It is the wound where Lunar City's polite history tore open, and the lights inside are not hope. They are preparation.
