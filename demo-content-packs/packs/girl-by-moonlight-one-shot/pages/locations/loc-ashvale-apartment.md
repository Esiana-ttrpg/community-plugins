# Ashvale Apartment

Ashvale Apartment is a mid-rise block on the quieter edge of [[loc-lunar-city]] — the sort of building that advertises security cameras and delivers them half-functional. [[family-ashvale]] has held the corner unit for three generations: narrow halls, a balcony facing the broadcast spire, a shoe rack that still carries sizes for relatives who stopped visiting when the moon-touched talk began. On paper it is a residence. In practice it is a vault for binding oaths and inherited shame.

[[pc-ren]] grew up here learning which drawers held medical supplies for nights when transformation outran language. The Ashvale bloodline carries [[lore-moon-curse]] not as scandal but as contract — promises made before the city renamed fear as policy. House records mention [[loc-old-shrine]] obliquely, the way families mention graves they cannot afford to visit. Ren's motivation to protect the coven begins in this kitchen, with whispered arguments about whether secrecy keeps siblings safe or condemns them to repeat [[event-founding-ritual]] without consent.

History in the flat is domestic and therefore sharp. After the [[event-recent-eclipse]], neighbors reported strange sounds that Ren's mother dismissed as plumbing while Ren counted minutes until dawn. [[org-lunar-directorate]] canvassers knocked with pamphlets about lunar wellness programs; Ren's grandmother refused the chain and kept the deadbolt drawn. [[org-crescent-resistance]] couriers use the service stair because the lobby camera favors angles that miss hoodies and hurry.

Conflict arrives as proximity. The apartment is not inside [[loc-shirogane-campus]] walls, which makes it safer from campus cameras and more exposed to city patrols. Regent Vale's Syndicate recruiters prefer targets who walk home alone after club hours. A **Herald reporter** once rang the buzzer asking about "Ashvale rumors"; the family learned how quickly [[org-city-herald]] attention becomes [[thread-city-secret]] pressure on a door without institutional backup.

Future pressure ties Ren's private war to the public one. Convergence oaths in family ledgers may activate whether Ren wants them or not. If the [[obj-lunar-idol]] is recovered, this balcony is close enough to see [[loc-ruined-observatory]] lights — close enough to know when leaving the apartment means choosing sides. Ashvale Apartment is small on a city map, but it is where the hidden war remembers that curses belong to people before they belong to factions.
