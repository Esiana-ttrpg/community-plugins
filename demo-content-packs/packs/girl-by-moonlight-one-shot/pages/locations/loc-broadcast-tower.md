# Broadcast Tower

Broadcast Tower lifts [[org-city-herald]] above the fog of [[loc-lunar-city]] — a red-lit mast visible from tram windows, from [[loc-shirogane-campus]] dormitories, from the Ashvale balcony where [[pc-ren]] sometimes watches the spire blink like a metronome for deadlines not yet written. What leaves this tower becomes what the city calls real: weather, transit, the soft voice telling parents their children are safe during lunar advisories.

History here is the history of narration. The Herald broke zoning scandals and transit graft when committees still answered to the Civic Watch name. It softened that edge after [[org-lunar-directorate]] consolidated licensing power, but never fully. The [[event-recent-eclipse]] split the newsroom: executives heard lawyers; reporters heard mothers on hold. [[quest-media-leak]] proved information still escapes these studios — and that escape has prices measured in careers, not just pages.

A **Herald reporter** keeps the sixteenth-floor beat that covers academy incidents and Directorate pressers. She knows which producers kill segments with a glance and which interns still believe journalism is a public service. Her motivation is simple and dangerous: print the [[thread-city-secret]] before officials finish renaming it. She trades protective anonymity with [[org-student-council]] contacts who trust her more than they trust their own minutes.

Conflict runs upward and downward. [[org-lunar-directorate]] liaisons arrive with gag orders dressed as national security guidance. Regent Vale's influence appears as legal threats against naming "unverified cults." [[org-crescent-resistance]] refuses cameras yet sometimes needs the tower's reach to warn families when Syndicate recruiters circle a neighborhood block.

Future pressure is the convergence broadcast. When [[event-future-convergence]] nears, the tower will air whatever script keeps transmitters licensed — unless someone inside decides the city deserves the truth live. The spire is not neutral ground. It is a weapon aimed at attention, and whoever controls the next nightly segment controls whether Lunar City enters the eclipse as citizens or as props in a binding Regent Vale intends to finish at [[loc-ruined-observatory]].
