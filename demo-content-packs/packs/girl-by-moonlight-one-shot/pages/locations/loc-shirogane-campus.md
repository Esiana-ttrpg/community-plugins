# Shirogane Campus

Shirogane Campus is the academy district of [[loc-lunar-city]] — a slope of lecture halls, dormitories, and club rooms arranged to look timeless while administrators replace security systems every eclipse season. [[org-shirogane-academy]] owns the map, but the district breathes like a small town: bakery queues at dawn, rooftop conversations after curfew, service tunnels students learn faster than faculty manuals. [[family-shirogane]] history is etched into donor plaques and scholarship names; walking here is walking through a family argument written in stone.

Before the [[event-recent-eclipse]], the campus hid its tensions behind festival banners and exam stress. Moon-touched changes were private tragedies handled by counselors who spoke in metaphors. Now the district carries a second geography — blind spots cataloged by [[org-crescent-resistance]], camera gaps noted by [[org-student-council]] President Elena, Syndicate recruiters who prefer stairwells near the astronomy wing. [[pc-hana]] knows every shortcut between the dorm where she sleeps and the classrooms where her sister **Sora** still tries to look ordinary.

Motivation across the district differs by role. Trustees want enrollment stability. Faculty want plausible deniability. Students want survival with diplomas attached. The [[haven-arcade-loft]] sits just off-campus edge, close enough to feel safe, far enough to deny official knowledge. [[pc-mira]] treats the district as evidence; [[pc-yuki]] treats it as a jurisdiction she must protect from her own employer.

Conflict sharpens whenever [[org-lunar-directorate]] extends curfew statutes or when [[org-city-herald]] vans park at the gate. A campus that once exported valedictorians now exports rumors about [[loc-ruined-observatory]] lights visible from the upper dormitories. House rivalries matter less than the question of who gets warned before the moon turns.

Future pressure is personal and public. Convergence calendars circulate in encrypted chats. If the Syndicate takes a student inside these walls — if Sora becomes a name instead of a sister — the campus will stop being a backdrop for the hidden war and become its battlefield. Shirogane Campus has always taught history. Soon it may teach whether the city repeats [[event-founding-ritual]] with a student body watching from the windows.
