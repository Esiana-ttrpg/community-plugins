# Lunar Idol

A fist-sized figure of pale stone veined with silver — warm to the touch on moonlit nights, cold under fluorescent lights. Shrine records describe it as the **keystone** of the first binding: the object that lets five bearers share the curse without tearing the city apart. The coven grew up on stories that called it protection. The eclipse proved it is also a **target**.

**Last confirmed sighting:** [[loc-old-shrine]], during the recent eclipse, at the center of the failed containment circle described in [[quest-failed-ritual]]. Security stills tied to [[quest-find-idol]] show the idol present until the feed glitches — then absent, with five silhouettes on the steps and no clear hand taking it.

**Current status:** missing. [[pc-hana]] is listed as the coven's custodial bearer in Resistance ledgers, but she does not have the physical relic. Three factions are actively searching:

- **[[org-crescent-resistance]]** — recover and reseal under coven oath before the curse settles district-wide.
- **[[org-lunar-directorate]]** — secure as regulated anomaly hardware; Yuki's handlers have a vault ready.
- **[[org-eclipse-syndicate]]** — steal and use at [[loc-ruined-observatory]] to force a new binding on Syndicate terms.

Trail fragments point down maintenance shafts from the shrine toward the observatory ley scar, but no faction has published proof of possession. Until someone does, every negotiation — Regent meetings, ruin delves, rooftop promises — is theater around an empty center.

See [[quest-find-idol]] and [[objective-recover-idol]].
