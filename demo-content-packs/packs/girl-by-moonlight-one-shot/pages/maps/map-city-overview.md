# Lunar City Map

This overview map is how the coven learned to see [[loc-lunar-city|Lunar City]] as more than a commute — a single sprawl where civic power, academy life, and buried ritual overlap until district lines feel like fiction. Pin the places that matter; read the gaps between them as carefully as the labels.

**Academy District** — [[loc-shirogane-campus|Shirogane Campus]] anchors the northeast quarter: dormitories, faculty archives, the commercial strip hiding [[haven-arcade-loft|Arcade Loft]], and the service stairwell up to [[haven-school-rooftop|School Rooftop]]. Most player-facing scenes start here because the [[org-shirogane-academy|Shirogane Academy]] and [[org-student-council|Student Council]] orbit this ground, and because campus cameras make every after-dark movement a small act of defiance.

**Civic Core** — [[loc-directorate-tower|Directorate Tower]] rises over the central grid, flanked by municipal offices and the cold geometry of legitimate surveillance. [[org-lunar-directorate|Lunar Directorate]] business radiates outward in permits, closures, and the language of scheduled relocations. [[pc-yuki|Yuki Arden]] knows this district by badge routes the rest of the coven only knows by rumor.

**Broadcast Ridge** — [[loc-broadcast-tower|Broadcast Tower]] sits on higher elevation west of center, close enough to the old observatory sightlines that [[project-repair-observatory|Repair the Observatory]] keeps a string on its transmission windows. Clips of the [[event-recent-eclipse|Recent Eclipse]] went public here first; whoever controls the ridge controls the story before [[org-city-herald|The City Herald]] can print it.

**Ashvale Quarter** — residential, outwardly calm, where [[loc-ashvale-apartment|Ashvale Apartment]] and [[haven-ashvale-apartment|Ashvale Apartment Haven]] sit three floors above ordinary commerce. [[family-ashvale|House Ashvale]] money blends into neighborhood routine until Syndicate surveillance turns the block into a pressure point.

**Ruin Belt** — southern and lower, where [[loc-ruined-observatory|Ruined Observatory]] slumps against the skyline and [[loc-old-shrine|Old Moon Shrine]] nestles in its shadow. Official maps call this district deferred development. The [[org-eclipse-syndicate|Eclipse Syndicate]], [[beast-moon-hound|Moon Hound]] sightings, and the empty vault that once held the [[obj-lunar-idol|Lunar Idol]] call it something else: the place the city's lie finally thins enough to see through.

Use this map at table scale, not atlas scale — one city, one curse, four girls measuring distances in check-ins and sirens rather than miles.
