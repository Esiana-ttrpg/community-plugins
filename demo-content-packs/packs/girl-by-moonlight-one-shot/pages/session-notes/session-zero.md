Three nights ago, the city held its breath for the [[event-recent-eclipse|Recent Eclipse]]. The moon did not simply darken — it *tilted*, as if something beneath [[loc-lunar-city|Lunar City]] had shifted weight. Every phone on [[loc-shirogane-campus|Shirogane Campus]] lit up at once with the same grainy clip: a silver figure on the old observatory roof, arms raised, halo fractured like cracked glass. By morning the [[obj-lunar-idol|Lunar Idol]] was gone from its shrine vault, and the [[org-lunar-directorate|Lunar Directorate]] called it a "scheduled relocation." Nobody believed them.

What happened next was worse than the theft. Students reported **strange sightings** — silhouettes that moved against the wind, reflections in dorm windows that blinked a half-second late, a voice on the PA system that laughed in a language the linguistics club couldn't parse. [[org-city-herald|The City Herald]] ran a breathless piece about "moon sickness." [[org-student-council|The Student Council]] demanded answers. [[org-crescent-resistance|The Crescent Resistance]] whispered that the curse was waking up. The [[lore-moon-curse|Moon Curse]] stopped being folklore and became the thing you checked for before walking home alone.

Four of us agreed to investigate before the factions tore the campus apart. [[pc-hana|Hana Shirogane]] couldn't stand another night watching her sister flinch at every siren. [[pc-mira|Mira Kaito]] had already filed three public-records requests the Directorate ignored. [[pc-yuki|Yuki Arden]] was caught between her agency badge and the friends she'd sworn to protect. [[pc-ren|Ren Ashvale]] said the coven needed a story the Resistance could control — or the Syndicate would write one for us. We didn't call ourselves heroes. We called it damage control.

Our first commitment: find the idol, or prove it was never really here. That became [[quest-find-idol|Find the Missing Idol]]. Our second: nobody acts alone after dark without a check-in. The rooftop haven at [[haven-school-rooftop|School Rooftop]] is our anchor — reinforced rail, shared key, no Directorate badges past the stairwell door unless Yuki vouches.

Before anyone rolled dice, we established **safety tools**. Lines: no sexualized harm to minors, no out-of-game betrayal played as "gotcha" twists, no forced corruption scenes without consent and an exit. Veils: gore stays off-screen; family abuse is implied, never performed. X-card and pause-anytime are live at the table. We also agreed that hope is a mechanic, not a punchline — [[thread-hope-vs-despair|Hope vs Despair]] is the spine of this campaign, and we won't let despair win by default.

If nobody intervenes, the Directorate seals the campus, the Resistance escalates to sabotage, and whatever took the idol learns our names. Session one starts here: four girls against a city that has been lying about the moon for a century. We're not ready. We're going anyway.
