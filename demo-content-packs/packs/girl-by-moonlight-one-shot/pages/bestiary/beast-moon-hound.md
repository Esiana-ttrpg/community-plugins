# Moon Hound

They name it in whispers because shouting draws its attention. The **Moon Hound** is not a dog in any kennel sense — it is a corrupted spirit shaped like one, assembled from eclipse residue and the grief of bindings that failed without ceremony. Witnesses on the [[loc-ruined-observatory|Ruined Observatory]] access road describe elongated shadow, breath that fogs glass in summer, and footsteps that arrive a beat after the thing that made them has already passed.

High threat is accurate. The Hound does not negotiate. It hunts along lunar sightlines, especially in the hours after the [[event-recent-eclipse|Recent Eclipse]], when the city's reflective surfaces still lag reality by that damning half-second. Students who fled the dome district report it moving *against* wind, climbing vertical rust where no weight should hold, pausing beneath broken telescope mounts as if listening for a frequency only [[anc-moon-touched|Moon-Touched]] blood can hear.

Habitat: ruins, but specifically the observatory's wounded geometry — collapsed catwalks, shattered lens housings, the stairwell down to [[loc-old-shrine|Old Moon Shrine]] where the [[obj-lunar-idol|Lunar Idol]] vault now gapes empty. Some Resistance scouts insist the Hound is a guard left behind when the idol walked. Others, including [[pc-mira|Mira Kaito]], think it is fallout — a spirit that should have been named and bound during the first ritual, now starving because someone broke the chain on purpose.

[[project-repair-observatory|Repair the Observatory]] slowed when volunteers stopped returning from dusk shifts. [[quest-investigate-ruins|Investigate the Ruins]] treats the Hound as evidence, not merely hazard: what it avoids, what it circles, what it will not cross may map the same containment geometry [[project-relic-research|Research Ancient Relics]] seeks in archives. [[pc-ren|Ren Ashvale]] refuses to discuss whether Ashvale oaths mention such spirits. [[pc-hana|Hana Shirogane]] refuses to let Sora within a mile of the site.

The Moon Hound is why the observatory stays ruin in official ledgers while every faction privately scrambles to control it. It is the city's guilt given teeth — and it has learned, lately, to hunt back.
