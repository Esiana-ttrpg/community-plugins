# Hana Shirogane

**Personal stake:** Her younger sister **Sora** is tied to the [[lore-moon-curse|Moon Curse]] — sleepwalking, humming vault tones, pinging near [[loc-old-shrine|Old Shrine]] minutes before the [[obj-lunar-idol|Lunar Idol]] vanished. Hana joined the coven to find the idol before the curse names Sora as its next vessel.

---

## What Happened

When the [[event-recent-eclipse|Recent Eclipse]] tilted the moon, Hana was in the [[family-shirogane|House Shirogane]] archive doing what her family always does: logging omens nobody else reads. The silver figure on the observatory roof wore a halo like cracked porcelain — the same image Sora sketched under her mattress before the Herald published. By dawn the idol was gone and the Directorate called it routine. Hana knew the vault alarm had sounded at 03:14. Sora's phone was at the shrine gate at 03:11.

## Who Wants What

- **Hana** wants Sora ordinary again — homework, bad tea, no halos.
- **[[org-shirogane-academy|Shirogane Academy]]** wants the vault scandal buried to protect endowment donors.
- **The coven** wants the truth Hana is still rationing.

## The Secret

Hana hasn't told the full coven about Sora's ping or sketch. On [[scene-rooftop-confession|Rooftop Confession]] she admitted the curse might be choosing her sister; she did not admit Sora might have opened the vault. The **Fifth Witness** blur in the rooftop photo matches Sora's height.

## If Nobody Intervenes

Sora becomes a Directorate "wellness case" or a Resistance symbol. Hana breaks first — corrupted form, fractured halo, family oath turned outward against the friends she recruited.
