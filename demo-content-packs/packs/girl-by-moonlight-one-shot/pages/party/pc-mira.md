# Mira Kaito

**Personal stake:** She/they will expose the [[org-lunar-directorate|Lunar Directorate]] cover-up around the [[obj-lunar-idol|Lunar Idol]] — or bury the city in subpoenas trying. The eclipse proved every official timeline is a lie; Mira intends to make lying expensive.

---

## What Happened

Mira was live-streaming the [[event-recent-eclipse|Recent Eclipse]] from the quad when the moon *wrong-turned* — not darkened, but skewed, like a lens cracking. Chat called it CGI. Mira saved the raw file. When the [[org-city-herald|City Herald]] ran the observatory silhouette, Mira matched frame timestamps to Directorate patrol logs and found a twenty-minute gap nobody would explain. She filed records requests before sunrise. By afternoon the council wanted her quiet. By nightfall she'd promised [[org-crescent-resistance|Crescent Resistance]] contacts a story — and promised the coven she'd hold the line one more day.

## Who Wants What

- **Mira** wants names, timestamps, and public accountability.
- **[[org-student-council|Student Council]]** wants stability for graduation season.
- **[[pc-yuki|Yuki Arden]]** wants Mira's evidence vetted before it becomes a raid warrant.

## The Secret

Mira recovered the **blurred rooftop photo** — five figures, four accounted for. She hasn't published it because Ren deleted the metadata and Hana looked like she'd shatter. Mira suspects the Fifth Witness is a Shirogane family member. She/they are one bad night from posting anyway.

## If Nobody Intervenes

The photo hits the Herald as "coven abduction ritual." Directorate locks [[loc-shirogane-campus|Shirogane Campus]]. Mira gets the scoop and loses the friends who trusted her with redactions.
