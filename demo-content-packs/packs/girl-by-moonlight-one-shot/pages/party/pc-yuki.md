# Yuki Arden

**Personal stake:** [[org-lunar-directorate|Directorate]] duty vs coven friendship — Yuki is the bridge everyone walks across until it creaks. If she chooses wrong, either her friends vanish into containment files or her career becomes a weapon pointed at them.

---

## What Happened

Yuki was on auxiliary patrol during the [[event-recent-eclipse|Recent Eclipse]], close enough to see the observatory silhouette flare and die. Her handler called it "atmospheric refraction." She logged refraction anyway and warned [[pc-hana|Hana]] an hour before the vault alarm went public. When the [[obj-lunar-idol|Lunar Idol]] disappeared, Yuki's superiors ordered campus calm. Yuki copied the patrol gap Mira found — and didn't report Mira for finding it.

## Who Wants What

- **Yuki** wants one true thing she can say in both uniforms.
- **The Directorate** wants the idol recovered without admitting the [[lore-moon-curse|Moon Curse]] is real.
- **[[org-eclipse-syndicate|Eclipse Syndicate]]** wants Yuki's access codes.

## The Secret

Yuki has seen internal memos naming "secondary vessels" — minors flagged for observation. Sora Shirogane's surname appears in a draft she hasn't shown Hana. Yuki tells herself she's buying time. The Blåhaj on her bed tells her she's scared.

## If Nobody Intervenes

Yuki files the compliant report. Hana's sister is picked up for "wellness." Yuki keeps her badge and loses the coven. Or she burns the badge first — and the Directorate paints her as Syndicate asset.
