# Ren Ashvale

**Personal stake:** [[org-crescent-resistance|Crescent Resistance]] operative vs coven protector — Ren must hide the [[lore-moon-curse|Moon Curse]] from outsiders long enough for the group to survive, without becoming the kind of handler who trades minors for leverage.

---

## What Happened

Ren was not on the quad for the [[event-recent-eclipse|Recent Eclipse]] — she was running a dead-drop near [[loc-ashvale-apartment|Ashvale Apartment]] when the sky broke. Resistance channels lit up with observatory footage before the Directorate scrubbed it. Ren's cell lead ordered retrieval of the [[obj-lunar-idol|Lunar Idol]] "by any means." Ren counter-ordered: **no Shirogane minors touched.** She meant it. She still made it to [[haven-school-rooftop|School Rooftop]] first on the night of the confession, because she knew Hana would climb alone.

## Who Wants What

- **Ren** wants the coven intact, the curse contained, and Resistance martyrdom off the menu.
- **Her cell** wants a win before [[org-eclipse-syndicate|Eclipse Syndicate]] claims the idol.
- **[[pc-hana|Hana]]** wants a sister, not an asset — and Ren wants to be worthy of that distinction.

## The Secret

Ren's handlers suggested feeding Hana partial truths to flush the vault thief. Ren refused — but someone in the network may have contacted Sora anyway. Ren cached the **Fifth Witness** photo and deleted its metadata to buy time. She hasn't told Hana about the handler memo. She tells herself protection and paternalism are different. The line blurs every eclipse.

## If Nobody Intervenes

Resistance publishes a false flag, Syndicate escalates, Directorate raids the dorm block. Ren extracts two coven members and loses two. Or she stays, burns her cover, and the Ashvale safehouse goes cold.
