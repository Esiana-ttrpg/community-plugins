# Directorate Secret Files

*(GM only — contradicts public lore.)*

The [[org-lunar-directorate]] did not inherit the moon-curse; it **inherited the cover-up**. Post-war ledgers in the tower vault show the old Civic Watch Committee purchasing shrine land, commissioning the first observatory dome, and hiring binders who swore an oath of silence. The public story — that the curse is a folk superstition damped by urban lighting — was focus-grouped in the 1960s when eclipse anomalies spiked and enrollment at [[org-shirogane-academy]] dropped.

**The first binding** was not a spiritual triumph. It was a **coerced ritual** documented in Annex 7-C: five adolescent participants, one of whom was administratively erased within a week of signing. The surviving names map cleanly to founding families still represented on the council. The erased name maps to nothing public — but internal geospatial tags place a sealed annex beneath [[loc-ruined-observatory]] labeled *Fifth Witness — Do Not Reactivate*.

Directorate analysts believe the lunar idol is not merely sacred; it is a **regulator** keyed to five concurrent bearers. Operating it with four active signatures causes harmonic bleed — exactly what the eclipse telemetry recorded. The Regent's office has known since before the coven formed that modern witness counts would not balance. They chose monitoring over disclosure because reopening the fifth seat would invalidate seventy years of liability law.

**Operational doctrine today:** let the Resistance believe it guards shrine knowledge; let the Syndicate pilfer enough to stay visible; intervene only to prevent civilian mass revelation. Yuki Arden was recruited as a **friendly witness** — close enough to the coven to report surge events, far enough to plausibly deny entrapment if she breaks cover.

Redacted eclipse reports still list **five witnesses** at [[loc-old-shrine]]. Field agents were told the fifth entry is "sensor ghost." Satellite review shows a living heat signature the coven did not account for.

**Hidden pact summary:** the Directorate will not prosecute bearer-active students who surrender the idol; the Resistance will not publish Annex 7-C; both sides agree the fifth name stays out of textbooks. The pact is fraying because someone stole the idol **during** a failed four-signature rite — proving the accord was always arithmetic performed on a lie.

If the party obtains these files, decide whether the Regent offers amendment (add a fifth bearer under Directorate supervision) or escalation (detain the coven until witness parity is restored). The Syndicate wants the opposite: publish the fifth name, break the pact, and invite the curse to run unbound so they can harvest transformations openly.
