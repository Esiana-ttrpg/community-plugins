Nine PM. Curfew soft enough that footsteps in the hall make everyone freeze. [[pc-yuki|Yuki]]'s dorm is small, neat, aggressively normal — textbooks stacked by color, Directorate pamphlet turned face-down on the desk, and on the bed a worn **Blåhaj** shark plushie propped against the pillow like it belongs on a recruitment poster. Yuki catches [[pc-mira|Mira]] looking and says, defensively, "It helps."

**What happened (before the scene):** The coven agreed to meet here after the [[scene-rooftop-confession|Rooftop Confession]] fallout — before the ruins run and before the Regent knows our names.

**Who wants what:**
- [[pc-hana|Hana]] wants permission to protect Sora without burning the group.
- Mira wants the vault logs on the table, every redaction explained.
- Yuki wants one hour where she is Yuki, not Agent Arden.
- Ren wants a plan that doesn't end with Resistance martyrs on the Herald front page.

They sit on the floor in a loose circle. Yuki passes around convenience-store onigiri like communion. Hana tries to joke about the Blåhaj "guardian spirit." The joke dies. Mira reads the stolen manifest numbers aloud anyway — 03:14 vault exit — and Hana's jaw tightens.

"It's going to be fine," Yuki says. Her hand finds the shark's fin. "We have each other. We have the rooftop. We have—" She almost says *the Directorate* and swaps to "—a head start."

Ren: "Say it like you mean it."

Yuki: "I'm trying."

Mira, softer than usual: "Trying counts. For tonight."

**The secret in the room:** Hana hasn't told them about Sora's 03:11 ping. The silence around it is louder than the harbor fog against the window.

**If nobody intervenes:** They leave separately, unchanged, and the ruins expedition becomes four solo missions. The Fifth Witness stays a blur in a deleted photo. Hope doesn't get a vote.

**Character beats:**
- Hana folds a paper crane from a snack wrapper — old nervous habit from [[family-shirogane|House Shirogane]] rituals.
- Mira updates the shared timeline on her phone, pronouns in the contact card corrected to she/they without comment.
- Yuki tucks her badge into the Blåhaj's mouth. Everyone pretends not to notice.
- Ren offers the last onigiri to Hana. "Eat. You can't carry your sister on an empty stomach."

The scene ends not with a plan, but with a promise: tomorrow they go to [[loc-ruined-observatory|Ruined Observatory]] together. Tonight they let "fine" be a future tense verb.
