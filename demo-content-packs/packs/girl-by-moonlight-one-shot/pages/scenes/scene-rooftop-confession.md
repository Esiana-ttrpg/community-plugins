## What Happened

Hana climbed the maintenance stairwell alone, vault manifest folded into her sleeve. Ren was already at the reinforced rail, hood up, Resistance sigil hidden under a campus lanyard. Neither had slept. The city below pulsed with harbor lights and the distant red sweep of [[loc-directorate-tower|Directorate Tower]] search beams.

Ren broke the silence first: "The coven thinks the Syndicate moved the idol. That's the story they'll print."

Hana: "And the true story?"

Ren didn't look at her. "Someone moved it so the Directorate couldn't seal the vault and declare martial law on campus. Someone who knew the combination."

The words landed like a confession aimed sideways. Hana's sister Sora had been humming the vault alarm tone in her sleep. Hana didn't say it. Ren didn't ask. The omission was the confession.

They argued — quietly, furiously — about whether [[org-crescent-resistance|Crescent Resistance]] handlers had fed Sora information. Ren swore on the crescent blade she hadn't authorized any contact with Shirogane family minors. Hana wanted to believe her. The [[thread-hope-vs-despair|Hope vs Despair]] thread tightened like a wire: if trust breaks here, the coven breaks everywhere.

At 03:16, a phone across the quad flashed — not theirs. Some student in the media club, maybe, shooting the eclipse aftermath. Later, Mira would recover a **blurred photo** from a class group chat: **five figures** on the rooftop, timestamp intact. Four they could name. The fifth was shorter, halo blur around the head, standing between Hana and the stairwell door — as if it had followed her up.

Ren deleted the EXIF and cached the image offline. "Fifth Witness," she said. Hana's breath hitched. The shape matched nothing in the vault logs except the word *vessel*.

Hana finally said her stake out loud: "If the [[lore-moon-curse|Moon Curse]] is choosing, it won't skip Sora just because I'm in the coven."

Ren covered Hana's cold hands with her jacket. "Then we find the [[obj-lunar-idol|Lunar Idol]] before the curse finds her."

Yuki arrived breathless with a Directorate patrol route; Mira arrived angry with a Herald tipster's number. The four of them didn't resolve anything. They agreed not to lie about the photo. They agreed not to publish it. They agreed to meet in Yuki's room before the ruins run.

## Who Wants What

- **Hana:** Keep Sora off every faction's board; recover the idol without confirming her worst fear.
- **Ren:** Protect the coven's narrative and Hana's heart — two jobs that may not fit in one pair of hands.

## The Secret

The Fifth Witness silhouette may be Sora — or something wearing her outline. The photo proves someone else was on the roof when Hana thought she was alone with Ren.

## If Nobody Intervenes

The blurred image leaks as "proof" of a coven kidnapping. Directorate locks [[loc-shirogane-campus|Shirogane Campus]]. Sora disappears into a "wellness interview" that doesn't end at dawn.
