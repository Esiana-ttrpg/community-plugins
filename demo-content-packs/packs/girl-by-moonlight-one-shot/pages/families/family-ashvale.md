# House Ashvale

House Ashvale is old money with older debts. Their name appears on donor plaques at [[org-shirogane-academy|Shirogane Academy]], on sealed minutes in the [[loc-directorate-tower|Directorate Tower]], and on the original charter that bound [[loc-lunar-city|Lunar City]] to lunar cycles it was never meant to survive. The family presents polished and untroubled. Behind [[loc-ashvale-apartment|Ashvale Apartment]], the truth lives in locked drawers and whispered across generations.

[[pc-ren|Ren Ashvale]] carries that truth in her blood. The Ashvale line is [[anc-moon-touched|Moon-Touched]] — not a metaphor, not a campus superstition, but a hereditary susceptibility the family documented long before the Directorate invented friendlier language for it. Moon-touched children were celebrated when the binding held. They are liabilities when it frays. Ren learned young which stories are for dinner parties and which are for the coven.

The **binding oaths** are the part outsiders never see. Each Ashvale heir swears to contain what the moon returns — to shield the city from the worst of the curse's hunger, to never speak the shrine names in public, to surrender personal desire when the family compact demands it. Ren swore like the rest. Then the [[obj-lunar-idol|Lunar Idol]] vanished, the [[event-recent-eclipse|Recent Eclipse]] tilted the sky, and the oath began to feel less like protection and more like a collar.

Ren's active burden is secrecy. The [[org-crescent-resistance|Crescent Resistance]] trusts her because Ashvale gold funded safe houses when official channels wouldn't. The [[org-eclipse-syndicate|Eclipse Syndicate]] watches because a moon-touched heir who falters is leverage. [[haven-ashvale-apartment|Ashvale Apartment]] — registered as a haven, threatened by rising surveillance — is both sanctuary and bait. Ren chose to hide the curse from outsiders; the cost is that she hides herself, too, even from friends who would stand with her if she let them.

House Ashvale survived by binding pain to purpose. Ren is not sure the compact survives the current moonfall. She is sure of one thing: if the family curse becomes public before the coven is ready, the Directorate will not offer mercy — only containment, and a press release about moon sickness that no one will believe anymore.
