# House Shirogane

For six generations, House Shirogane has kept the [[org-shirogane-academy|Shirogane Academy]] archives lit through coups and civic lies. They were never nobles in the Ashvale sense — no binding oaths carved into marble — but the family name opens faculty doors and closes dorm conversations when someone asks what the observatory used to track.

[[pc-hana|Hana Shirogane]] inherited that reputation along with the keys to a rooftop haven and the unspoken expectation that Shirogane daughters do not break. She has honored both, until now. Her younger sister **Sora** was never meant to carry the line; she was the quiet one who sketched lunar phases in the margins of history primers and slept through the political dinners Hana endured. Then came the [[event-recent-eclipse|Recent Eclipse]].

Sora remembers it with a precision that frightens the adults around her. Not the public clip — the silver figure, the fractured halo — but the *interval* between darkness and return: iron taste, the shadow angle on [[loc-old-shrine|Old Moon Shrine]] steps, a syllable tutors insist has no phonetic record. Other witnesses forgot by morning. Sora's notebook entries grow less like observation and more like prophecy.

The [[lore-moon-curse|Moon Curse]] is not folklore anymore. Sora's hands tremble when sirens pass; the infirmary calls it stress. Hana knows the cadence of a worsening condition — the same cadence their grandmother described before she stopped attending classes.

Hana's dilemma has no clean name. The coven needs her on the [[quest-find-idol|Find the Missing Idol]] trail and the [[haven-school-rooftop|School Rooftop]] watch rotation. Sora needs her home before curfew, needs someone to believe the eclipse was not a broadcast glitch, needs protection from a city that devours girls who see too clearly. Every hour Hana spends translating archive fragments with [[pc-mira|Mira Kaito]] is an hour Sora spends alone in a dorm where the windows blink half a second late. Every hour at Sora's bedside is an hour the [[org-eclipse-syndicate|Eclipse Syndicate]] learns another name.

House Shirogane built its legacy on scholarship and discretion. Hana is learning that family, for once, refuses to wait its turn behind duty.
