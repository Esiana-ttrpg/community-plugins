# The Hidden Pact

Before the coven formed in the open, someone signed on the city's behalf. [[lore-directorate-secret]] files describe a post-war accord: the [[org-lunar-directorate]] would manage eclipse anomalies publicly while the Resistance guarded shrine knowledge — and both sides agreed never to speak about the fifth seat at the first binding.

**Why now?** The failed ritual cracked a seal the pact was meant to maintain. Directorate analysts are comparing modern witness counts to the founding roster. If the accord unravels, Yuki must choose between her friends and her badge in a single meeting.

**Who cares?** The Regent still holds the original countersignature. Ren's mentors believe the pact was a betrayal dressed as peace. Mira does not know her grandmother's name appears on an annex page the party has not found yet.

**What if we fail?** The city returns to open faction war. The Syndicate stops pretending to be a rumor. And the erased fifth participant from [[event-founding-ritual]] finally gets a funeral — or a throne.
