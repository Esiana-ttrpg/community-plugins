# Investigate the Ruins

[[loc-ruined-observatory]] keeps coughing up eclipse-era debris — cracked lens housings, Directorate seal wax, and chalk circles that match the shrine theft. Resistance scouts swear someone is living in the collapsed dome again. Ren wants to go in before the Syndicate booby-traps the tunnels; Yuki has a warrant she has not yet signed.

**Why now?** The idol trail dead-ends at the shrine. Every faction that lost the relic is converging on the ruins because the observatory sits on the same ley scar as [[loc-old-shrine]]. If the thief hid or moved the idol through the old maintenance shafts, the proof is down there — along with whatever the Syndicate is building in the dark.

**Who cares?** [[pc-ren]] treats the ruins as sacred Resistance ground. [[pc-mira]] needs hard evidence for the council hearing she has been stalling. Hana keeps dreaming of a girl in a cracked mirror room beneath the dome — a face that is not any of them.

**What if we fail?** The Syndicate seals the site, the Directorate cordons it, and the coven loses its last chance to learn who took the idol before [[quest-confront-regent]] becomes the only move left.
