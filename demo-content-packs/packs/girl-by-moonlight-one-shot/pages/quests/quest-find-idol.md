# Find the Missing Idol

The lunar idol vanished from [[loc-old-shrine]] during the eclipse — the same night the coven's binding cracked and Hana's sister woke screaming. [[pc-mira]] pulled the security stills from the academy annex: five silhouettes on the shrine steps, not four. One figure stands where no coven member admits to standing, then the feed glitches and the idol is simply gone.

**Why now?** Without the idol, the moon-curse bleeds through civilian masks faster each night. The Directorate has declared a curfew sweep; the [[org-eclipse-syndicate]] is already digging under the ruined observatory. Every hour the relic stays missing, someone else learns what the girls are.

**Who cares?** [[pc-hana]] needs it to stabilize her sister. [[pc-ren]] swears the Resistance can turn possession of the idol into leverage against the Directorate. [[pc-yuki]] says her agency handlers want it catalogued — and will seize it if the coven cannot produce a lawful chain of custody. Even [[org-student-council]] is asking questions Mira cannot dodge forever.

**What if we fail?** The curse settles into the city permanently. The Syndicate completes whatever ritual they started on the eclipse roof. And that fifth silhouette on the footage becomes a sixth witness — or the first body the coven has to explain.
