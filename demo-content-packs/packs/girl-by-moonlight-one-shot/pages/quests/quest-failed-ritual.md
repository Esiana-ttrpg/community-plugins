# Stabilize the Failed Ritual

On the night of the eclipse the coven attempted a containment rite at [[loc-old-shrine]] — four girls, one oath, the lunar idol at the center. The moon answered wrong. Light bent inward, Hana collapsed, and the idol shattered its binding circle and vanished before anyone could finish the closing verse.

**Why now?** The ritual was supposed to buy a season of peace — to push the curse back long enough to find a permanent fix. Instead it announced to every sensitive ear in the city that the shrine was active again.

**Who cares?** Hana's sister felt the backlash in her sleep. The Resistance elders who taught Ren the rite are furious. Yuki's handlers recorded an anomaly spike they will not explain away.

**What if we fail?** *(They failed.)* The curse is loose, the idol is missing, and the coven cannot agree whether someone sabotaged the circle or whether the rite was always meant for five bearers, not four. Whatever comes next — [[quest-find-idol]], the ruins, the Regent — grows from this broken night.
