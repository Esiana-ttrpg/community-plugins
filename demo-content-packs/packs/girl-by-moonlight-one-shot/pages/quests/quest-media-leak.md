# Stop the Media Leak

[[org-city-herald]] almost ran a segment on "moon-touched students" after a rooftop phone video surfaced — shaky footage of silver light over [[haven-school-rooftop]], a voice that might have been Hana's. Mira spent three nights in the broadcast tower stairwells, leaning on council contacts and old favors, until the story died in editorial.

**Why now?** The eclipse made civilians look up. Every phone in Lunar City has a partial clip of the shrine blackout. One honest headline and the coven's double lives end before the idol is recovered.

**Who cares?** [[pc-mira]]'s reputation on the student council was the shield. Ren would have burned the tower down before letting names print. Yuki's Directorate badge would have meant arrest warrants by morning.

**What if we fail?** *(Resolved — barely.)* The Herald held the piece, but the raw files still exist on a freelancer's drive. The Syndicate knows the coven can be pressured through publicity now. This victory bought time, not silence.
