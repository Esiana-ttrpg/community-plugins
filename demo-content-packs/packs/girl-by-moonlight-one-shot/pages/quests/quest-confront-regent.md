# Confront the Regent

The Regent of [[org-lunar-directorate]] has summoned no one publicly since the eclipse — but private channels say they will receive "student representatives" who can explain the shrine incident. Yuki has the appointment slot. Mira has the questions. Ren has a blade he is not supposed to bring to [[loc-directorate-tower]].

**Why now?** Ruin evidence points upward: Directorate seal wax on observatory debris, curfew orders timed to cover idol movements, and a redacted witness list that names five girls when the coven insists there were only four on the steps.

**Who cares?** [[pc-yuki]] can open the tower door or become its prisoner. Hana needs the Regent to admit what the Directorate knows about the curse and the missing fifth name. The Resistance wants a confession on record before the Syndicate trades the idol for amnesty.

**What if we fail?** The Regent declares the coven a hazard and hands custody to Syndicate "recovery teams." Yuki is recalled to active duty against her friends. The idol becomes state property — and the fifth witness stays buried in the files forever.
