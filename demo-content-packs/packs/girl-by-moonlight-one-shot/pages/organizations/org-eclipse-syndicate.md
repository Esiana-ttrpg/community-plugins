# Eclipse Syndicate

Before the [[event-recent-eclipse]], the Eclipse Syndicate was a story told to frighten freshmen — a shadow order that met under dead stars and promised power to girls who had already begun to change. Faculty at [[org-shirogane-academy]] filed the rumors under urban legend. City editors at [[org-city-herald]] never found a masthead. Even [[org-lunar-directorate]] analysts listed the name beside hoax cults and copycat graffiti. The eclipse ended that comfort. Surveillance footage from [[loc-ruined-observatory]] showed figures in mirrored masks moving through smoke, and within a week three students vanished from campus perimeter walks.

The Syndicate's visible face is **Regent Vale**, a title more than a person: a regent who speaks for the order when it chooses to speak at all. Vale's agents do not preach in alleys anymore. They recover **lunar relics** — fragments tied to the [[event-founding-ritual]] and the missing [[obj-lunar-idol]] — and ferry them toward [[loc-old-shrine]] before the next convergence foretold in [[event-future-convergence]]. Vale treats the [[org-crescent-resistance]] as thieves of an inheritance the Syndicate claims by right of transformation, not citizenship.

Headquarters nominally lie in the [[loc-ruined-observatory]], though the burned dome is only the public mask. Vale's true cells rotate through service tunnels and sealed annexes the Directorate maps poorly on purpose. The Syndicate's motivation is not chaos for its own sake: they believe the [[lore-moon-curse]] is a birthright being suppressed, and that whoever controls the Binding sites when the moon falls again will rewrite who counts as human in [[loc-lunar-city]].

Pressure is mounting on every side. [[pc-yuki]]'s Directorate handlers want arrests without explanations. [[pc-mira]]'s council contacts whisper that campus security logs were altered the night of the eclipse. [[quest-confront-regent]] hangs over the coven like a blade — confront Vale too early and the Syndicate scatters; wait too long and the relic trail goes cold. The city's hidden war is no longer hidden. It has a regent, a deadline, and a sky that remembers how to go dark.
