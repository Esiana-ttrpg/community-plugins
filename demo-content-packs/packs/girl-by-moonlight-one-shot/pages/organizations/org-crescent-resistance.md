# Crescent Resistance

The Crescent Resistance was never a banner hung in a square. It grew from oath-bound circles — girls who survived first transformations together and refused to let the [[lore-moon-curse]] become a recruitment pitch for predators. For years the network looked like study groups, late-night arcade crews, and the kind of friendship adults dismiss as phase. [[pc-ren]] inherited that tradition from [[family-ashvale]] binding oaths; today the Resistance is the reason a moon-touched student can vanish from a Directorate interview and reappear at morning homeroom with a plausible alibi.

Operational headquarters sit above a shuttered arcade on the edge of [[loc-shirogane-campus]] — the [[haven-arcade-loft]], known only to trusted cells. From there the Resistance maps Syndicate movements, caches medical supplies for unstable transformations, and runs the [[quest-investigate-ruins]] pipeline that keeps the coven ahead of official silence. Members believe [[loc-ruined-observatory]] hides a Binding Ritual site beneath the fire-scarred dome: not mere graffiti, but stone laid in the same era as [[event-founding-ritual]]. [[project-repair-observatory]] is controversial inside the Resistance itself — some want the ruins sealed forever; others argue the city will repeat history if no one reads what is written there.

Conflict defines the Resistance because secrecy is expensive. [[org-lunar-directorate]] raids under eclipse emergency statutes have picked off peripheral contacts. [[org-city-herald]] reporters circle campus tragedies with questions the Resistance cannot answer without exposing [[pc-hana]]'s circle. Worst of all, Regent Vale's Syndicate agents offer power with less shame than the Resistance offers fear — and adolescent despair listens.

The future pressure is convergence. The Resistance knows the [[obj-lunar-idol]] is not a trophy; it is a key. If Vale reaches the shrine first, the next binding will not be a myth in [[lore-directorate-secret]] files — it will be policy. The coven must choose whether to remain a shield for students like Ren or become something sharper before [[event-future-convergence]] turns [[loc-lunar-city]] into an altar again.
