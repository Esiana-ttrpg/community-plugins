# Student Council

The Student Council is the polite face of [[org-shirogane-academy]] politics — budgets for club festivals, mediation between dormitories, the performance of student voice that keeps trustees confident the campus governs itself. Its chambers sit inside the academy proper, but its power is borrowed. Every resolution passes through faculty review; every president learns quickly which grievances may be spoken aloud and which must be carried down fire stairs to someone who will not put them in minutes.

**Council President Elena** runs this term with surgical calm. She inherited a council fractured by the [[event-recent-eclipse]]: attendance spikes, unexplained absences, security cameras "upgraded" overnight. Elena speaks on the morning announcements about unity while privately maintaining a ledger of students who returned from moonlit walks changed. She did not ask for [[pc-mira]]'s crusade against the Directorate, but she has not blocked it either — a distinction that keeps Mira in an armband instead of a suspension file.

History here is recent and sharp. Before the eclipse, the council fought mundane wars: funding for the literature club, noise complaints against the arcade district. Afterward, the council became a pressure valve. When [[org-lunar-directorate]] demanded expanded curfews, Elena negotiated wording that sounds compliant and reads loophole. When [[org-city-herald]] reporters camped at the gates, she fed them enough truth to prevent a panic and enough omission to protect names.

Conflict follows because the council sits between institutions that do not share goals. The academy wants scandal contained; the Directorate wants names; the Resistance wants council keys to service tunnels. Elena balances on a wire: too much transparency invites Syndicate recruiters; too much silence makes the council complicit in whatever Regent Vale prepares at [[loc-ruined-observatory]].

Future pressure is enrollment season under a darkening calendar. Parents are reading eclipse headlines. The council must decide whether to formalize a student safety pact — publicly — or keep protecting moon-touched classmates through back channels. [[pc-hana]]'s worry for her sister **Sora**, still an underclassman without a transformation story, makes the choice personal for the whole coven. When [[event-future-convergence]] nears, the council will either become a shield with a stamp or break into factions no president can reunite from a podium.
