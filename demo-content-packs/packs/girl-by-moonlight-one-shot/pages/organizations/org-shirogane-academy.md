# Shirogane Academy

Shirogane Academy is the city's polished excuse for hope — ivy on concrete, astronomy prizes, enrollment brochures that never mention the [[lore-moon-curse]]. Its grounds span [[loc-shirogane-campus]], a district where tram bells overlap with club room laughter and, on bad nights, the howl of something that should not be near a dormitory window. [[family-shirogane]] lineage runs through the school's founding scholarships; [[pc-hana]] walks those halls as both student and inheritor of a name the trustees invoke when donors ask about tradition.

History here is layered. The academy predates the Directorate's glass tower and outlasted the Civic Watch Committee's first attempts to catalog "lunar sickness" as truancy. Faculty once taught the [[event-founding-ritual]] as myth in literature seminars — cautionary poetry, not civic engineering. After the [[event-recent-eclipse]], the same myths appear on bathroom mirrors written in handwriting that is not quite human. Headmasters respond with counseling referrals and camera upgrades that never quite cover the service tunnels [[org-crescent-resistance]] uses.

Motivation at the institutional level is survival without transformation of reputation. The academy needs [[org-lunar-directorate]] funding and trustee confidence. It needs [[org-student-council]] to absorb student rage so scandals stop at the gate. It needs to believe [[org-eclipse-syndicate]] stories are urban legends because the alternative — that predators recruit inside its walls — would empty classrooms in a semester.

Conflict is intimate. **Sora**, Hana's younger sister, still attends classes without the visible changes older moon-touched students hide. Her presence makes every warning about Syndicate recruitment a family argument, not an abstract threat. [[pc-mira]]'s investigations scrape paint off the academy's respectable facade. [[pc-ren]]'s Resistance ties mean the school hosts both prey and protectors, often in the same homeroom.

Future pressure is convergence enrollment. Parents read [[org-city-herald]] headlines and ask whether Shirogane is safe. The academy must choose transparency that could kill it or complicity that could kill students. When [[event-future-convergence]] nears, the campus will not be a neutral backdrop — it will be the place where the city's hidden war decides which girls become weapons, witnesses, or sacrifices tied to [[obj-lunar-idol]] and the ruins beyond the hill.
