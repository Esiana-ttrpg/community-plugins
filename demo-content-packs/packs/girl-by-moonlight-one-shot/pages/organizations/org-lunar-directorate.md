# Lunar Directorate

The Lunar Directorate is what [[loc-lunar-city]] calls government when it wants to sound futuristic instead of frightened. Its glass seat in [[loc-directorate-tower]] broadcasts calm: transit schedules, eclipse safety advisories, scholarships for gifted students at [[org-shirogane-academy]]. Older residents still say **Civic Watch Committee** — the name before reform — and some file boxes in the basement still carry that letterhead beside red stamps reading SEALED. Publicly the Directorate exists to manage civic order during lunar events. Privately it manages **memory**.

[[pc-yuki]] walks both sides of that line. Her badge opens doors at the tower and closes them on friends who ask too many questions. Directorate analysts tracked the [[event-recent-eclipse]] with instruments the Resistance only hears about secondhand. Their internal summaries disagree with what [[org-city-herald]] published by omission, not error. The party knows pieces of [[lore-moon-curse]]; the Directorate knows [[lore-directorate-secret]] — including the uncomfortable claim that the first binding was engineered, not discovered.

Motivation at the top is stability at any cost. Eclipse syndicates are officially denied even while covert channels negotiate with Regent Vale's envoys: better to chart a monster's path than pretend monsters are legends. Against [[org-crescent-resistance]], the Directorate prefers arrests that look like truancy interventions. Against the academy, it prefers compliance — sealed dormitory logs, counselors who report moon-sickness symptoms, and student council presidents who can be reminded where funding originates.

The conflict is structural. The Directorate cannot admit the curse predates the city without unraveling land claims, insurance law, and a century of textbooks. Yet every eclipse widens the gap between broadcast reassurance and rooftop truth. [[quest-media-leak]] proved leaks still happen; [[thread-city-secret]] proves the city remembers what officials delete.

Future pressure arrives with [[event-future-convergence]]. Emergency powers that felt temporary after the last eclipse are already written into permanent statute drafts. If the coven forces a public reckoning, the Directorate must choose between confession and crackdown — and [[loc-directorate-tower]] has rehearsed both scripts before the party was born.
